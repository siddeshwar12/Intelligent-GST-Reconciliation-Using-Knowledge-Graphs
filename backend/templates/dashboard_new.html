📤 Step 1: Seller Files GSTR-1

What Happens:
The seller (supplier) files their GSTR-1 return with the government, declaring all outward supplies (sales) made during the month.

Information Included:
• Invoice numbers and dates
• Buyer GSTIN
• Invoice amounts
• GST charged (CGST, SGST, IGST)

Why It Matters:
This is the source of truth. If a seller doesn't file GSTR-1 or files incorrect information, the buyer cannot claim ITC.

×

🏛️ Step 2: Government Generates GSTR-2B

What Happens:
The government automatically generates GSTR-2B for each buyer based on the GSTR-1 filed by all their suppliers.

Key Point:
The buyer CANNOT modify GSTR-2B. It is a read-only document generated by the government system.

What It Contains:
• All invoices where the buyer's GSTIN appears
• ITC eligible amounts
• Supplier details

Why It Matters:
GSTR-2B is the government's official record of what ITC the buyer is eligible to claim. Any claim beyond this is invalid.

×

💼 Step 3: Buyer Claims ITC in GSTR-3B

What Happens:
The buyer files their GSTR-3B return and claims Input Tax Credit based on their GSTR-2B.

The Problem:
Some buyers claim MORE ITC than what appears in their GSTR-2B, either by mistake or intentionally.

Example:
• GSTR-2B shows: ₹1,800 ITC eligible
• Buyer claims in GSTR-3B: ₹2,200
• Difference: ₹400 (INVALID CLAIM)

Why It Matters:
Invalid ITC claims lead to penalties, interest, and potential fraud charges. Our system catches these mismatches.

×

🔍 Step 4: System Verifies Full Chain

What Our System Does:
We validate the COMPLETE chain using Knowledge Graph technology:

Validation Steps:
1. Check if invoice exists in seller's GSTR-1
2. Verify invoice appears in buyer's GSTR-2B
3. Compare amounts (invoice vs GSTR-1 vs GSTR-2B)
4. Verify GST calculations are correct
5. Check if seller's GSTIN is valid and active
6. Detect fraud patterns (circular trading, fake invoices)
7. Assess vendor compliance risk using ML

Why It's Different:
Traditional systems only match invoice numbers. We validate the ENTIRE relationship network using graph database technology.
