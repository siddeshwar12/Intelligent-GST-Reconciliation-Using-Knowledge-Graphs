📋 What is GSTR-1?

GSTR-1 is a monthly return filed by registered sellers. It contains details of all outward supplies (sales) made during the month. When a seller files GSTR-1, this data becomes the foundation for the buyer's Input Tax Credit (ITC) claim.

🔄 What is GSTR-2B?

GSTR-2B is an auto-generated statement by the government. It shows all purchases made by a buyer based on the GSTR-1 filed by their suppliers. This is the buyer's eligible ITC that they can claim. The buyer CANNOT modify this document - it's purely informational.

💰 What is GSTR-3B?

GSTR-3B is the monthly return where the buyer claims their Input Tax Credit. The buyer enters the ITC amount they want to claim. Our system compares this claimed amount with the eligible amount from GSTR-2B. Any mismatch is flagged for investigation.
